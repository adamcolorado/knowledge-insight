Course 3 · Module 1

Safety and Alignment

This module covers the argument that advanced AI could be hard to control, and the evidence offered for and against it. You'll be able to explain the alignment problem, judge what laboratory safety tests do and don't show, and compare forecasts that run from catastrophe to abundance to ordinary technological change. The module gives you the terms of a debate in which well-informed people disagree sharply.

Module objectives

  • Explain the alignment problem and why it is hard to give an AI system the goals you intend.
  • Assess what safety evaluations and interpretability research do and don't show about current models.
  • Compare the main forecasts about advanced AI and identify the assumptions on which they differ.
